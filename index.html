Privacy Policy for Lexora Worksheet

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Lexora Worksheet ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Lexora Worksheet mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User identifier
  • Teacher or student account information
  • Worksheet scan images
  • Worksheet answers and analysis results
  • Technical information required for application functionality and security

We do not collect information that is not required for the operation of the App.

2. Camera and Document Scanning

Lexora Worksheet may request access to the device camera in order to scan worksheets and documents.

Camera access is used only when the user chooses to scan a document. The App does not access the camera in the background.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected by the App may be used to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Provide worksheet scanning and analysis
  • Display worksheet and student results
  • Maintain application functionality
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized access or abuse
  • Provide technical support

We do not sell users' personal information.

4. Data Storage and Processing

Lexora Worksheet may use secure cloud infrastructure to store and process application data.

Some application services may be provided through third-party infrastructure providers, including Supabase.

Data transmitted between the App and our services is protected using secure network connections where applicable.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services including:

  • Supabase
  • Google ML Kit
  • Google Play Services

These providers may process limited information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Information may only be shared:

  • With service providers required to operate the App
  • When required by law
  • To protect the security and integrity of the App
  • With authorized educational organizations where applicable

7. Children's Privacy

Lexora Worksheet may be used in educational environments involving students.

Where the App is used by minors, accounts and data should be managed in accordance with applicable child privacy and education laws and, where required, with authorization from a parent, guardian, school, or educational institution.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

8. Data Retention

Personal and worksheet-related data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the App's services or to comply with legal and operational requirements.

9. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their personal information and associated account data.

To request data deletion, contact us using the email address provided below.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
